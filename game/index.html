Last Hand Standing

A low-poly zombie survival shooter you play with your hands. One hand is the gun, the other walks you around. Survive as many waves as you can.

Gun hand

Make a finger gun and point to aim. Lift your thumb, then drop it onto your index finger to fire. Make a fist to reload. Aim at the screen edges to turn.

Move hand

Open your palm: where you open it is the centre. Move it up to walk forward, down to back off, sideways to strafe. Fist to stop. Flick it up quickly to jump.

Phone

Make an OK sign with your move hand (or hold Q) to doom-scroll: you heal, but you can't shoot. Move the pinch to scroll.

Find

Six music discs glow in the fog: walk over one to play its track. From wave 2 an alpha may start dancing somewhere. Follow its beat and put it down before the horde grows.

Gun hand

The camera feed stays on your device; hand tracking runs in your browser. Keyboard: WASD, mouse to aim, click to shoot, Space to jump, R to reload, hold Q for the phone.

Calibrate

  • Show your gun hand as a finger gun
  • Drop your thumb to fire once
  • Show your move hand with the palm open
  • Move the open palm up to walk

Hold your gun hand where it feels natural to aim straight ahead: that becomes the centre of the screen.
