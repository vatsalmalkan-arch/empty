Last Hand Standing

The forest went grey and the dead got up. Hold out as long as you can: every wave you survive, pick an upgrade. Find the six lost records, and if you hear a beat in the fog, an alpha is dancing up a horde. Put it down.

Keyboard & mouse

WASD move · Shift sprint · Space jump · Click shoot · Right click aim · R reload · F shove · hold Q phone · Esc pause

Hands (webcam)

Gun hand: point to aim, drop your thumb to fire, fist to reload. Move hand: open palm is a joystick, fist stops, flick up to jump, OK sign takes out the phone.

Phone

Doom-scrolling heals you, but you can't shoot and you walk slowly. Pick your moment.

Drops

The dead sometimes drop ammo (yellow) or a medkit (green). Ammo is limited, so keep moving and keep collecting.
